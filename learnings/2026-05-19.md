## What surprised me
- The skill surfaced prompt caching as a 60-75% cost lever unprompted. I hadn't priced caching into the cost math. At 200k/day this changes whether the feature is profitable.
- It proposed a two-tier internal cascade (Haiku → Sonnet on low confidence → orchestrator escalation) when I had only described the orchestrator escalation. Genuinely better design than what I had in my head.
- It flagged vendor concentration (Haiku + Sonnet are both Anthropic) and recommended running Gemini Flash Lite through the eval now so switching is config, not engineering. The kind of trade-off most PMs surface only after an incident.

## What this taught me about AI PM thinking
1. Prompt caching is a primary cost lever at scale, not an optimization. It changes the model-selection answer.
2. Confidence calibration is the load-bearing assumption in any cascade. The 0.80 threshold is a guess until measured.
3. Vendor concentration is a trade-off you own from day one, not a problem you discover later.
